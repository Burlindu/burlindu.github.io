Firmitas, Utilitas, Venustus.

Durability, Function and Beauty.

(Vetruvius, circa 50 BCE)

Translation of Vitruvius often mis-translates even these 3 basics. They are an expression of professionalism in the field of design, even before it existed as a proper profession as such. The key is not allowing any one of these 3 components to over-ride and allow another to be forgotten. Beauty with Utility, Longevity with Function, and Durability with superior Aesthetics.

In construction it is often noted that the difficulty in completing building projects calls for a selection of 'Quality, Time, Price: pick two'. Vitruvius' dictum tells us, except perhaps in exceptional circumstances when the program specifically says otherwise, that in order to be a proper professional one must deliver on all three cournerstones of architectural design: Firmitas, Utilitas & Venustus.
