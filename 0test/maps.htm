Maps

The new (2026) Biidaasige Park in the Portlands did not seem to have available proper user maps, and Google maps of course is not yet up to date on the area.

So I downloaded a wayfinding map PDF from Reddit (a city map for physical on-site wayfinding signs), spun it so N is at the top and added some new stuff, like the East-West Shuttle Dock.

2026
- There are lots of boating landings, but no car spot to drop off a canoe or board, as far as I can tell. The one parking lot is across from the OWL, but is noted as not being that big (I am of course not advocating lots of parking - but I'd like a boat drop-off/pick-up spot)
- Keating Channel I think is not navigable, so will not work yet as a boating loop
- Word is there is going to be boat (Canoe/SUP) rental on site, I think in the west end by Canoe Cove. Oh that would be awesome
- In mid summer the new trees are not big enough for much shade. It's crazy hot. A good place to catch cool shade is that looping path under the Commissioner's bridge, in the East.
