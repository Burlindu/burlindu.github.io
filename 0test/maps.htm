The Mubwaka trip (John, Liam and I) was a great trip and the site indicated with the stick was awesome. However the map was a problem. John had not brought a map and I printed maps the day before - however the printer was low on ink and only a faint outline showed. So we hand coloured and re-defined the lines by hand the night before. This resulted in an error - I drew a V inlet at the drop-in point, but it was just a double lined road. This caused a problem. I thought we were starting in a narrow bay/inlet, when in fact we were already out facing the main channel. My distance bearings were then way off, as well as direction relative to teh far shoreline.

So when we launched I directed us to launch hugging the right shore, and then we would head out left across to the portage. But we were already facing the bay, and should have just gone straight out, tending the left shore if anything, to hit that portage on the north end. Hugging the right shore, which would have got us out of little bay/inlet, instead took us south, in the direction.

So we launched following the shore on our right, instead of on our left, and went South instead of North. We lost 3 hours - and one small portage (a sign on the portage telling us we were on the wrong lake - Little Eagle Lake - clued us in to our error, although by that point we knew we were well off somehow).

That long water inlet where "IN/OUT" is indicated is not a water inlet at all - it's the road. I traced it wrong, John brought no map, yadda yadda yadda, However we still made it to out intended site that night, not so much a big deal in the end. Quality maps are important.
